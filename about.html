THE ORIGIN STORY

Sculptural Simplicity

Bespoke Heavyweight Fits

AURA was established in 2024 to disrupt fast-fashion trends. By focusing exclusively on fundamental silhouettes—the T-shirt and the Hoodie—we seek perfection through micro-iterations in weight, ribbing, and dye processes.

Our pattern blocks are built with a signature boxy structural drop-shoulder. Every seam is double-locked to ensure durability and resistance to deformation, allowing a loose fit that looks sharp and deliberate.

Organic Sourcing

We collaborate exclusively with certified organic cotton farms that prioritize soil preservation and water loop recycling. Our heavy loopback French terrys and carded Pima fibers are spun chemical-free.

We dye in small batches using non-toxic natural oxides and low-impact pigments, giving each shade a organic, rich depth that develops unique wash characteristics over time.
